I am a Senior Deep Learning Software Engineer at [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/).

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from the University of Washington in January 2026, where I worked with [Prof. Jenq-Neng Hwang](https://people.ece.uw.edu/hwang/) and the [Information Processing Lab](https://ipl-uw.github.io/). Prior to that, I received my M.S. degree in ECE at University of Washington (2021) and B.S. degree in EE at National Taiwan University (2019). I worked with [Prof. Yu-Chiang Frank Wang](http://vllab.ee.ntu.edu.tw/members.html) at the [Vision and Learning Lab](http://vllab.ee.ntu.edu.tw/) on computer vision.

My research interests lie around **Visual Object Tracking**, **Multi-Object Tracking (Single-View, Multi-View, Cross-View)**, and **Vision-Language Models (VLMs)**.
